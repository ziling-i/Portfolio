# iSpeed 2.0 Application Enhancement

**Role:** Business Analyst
**Industry:** FinTech
**Project Type:** Application Enhancement

---

## Project Overview

**iSpeed 2.0** was an application enhancement initiative focused on modernizing the existing trading application experience, improving information presentation, introducing new functionality, and simplifying selected user workflows.

Within the broader iSpeed 2.0 enhancement project, I was primarily responsible for the requirements analysis and functional analysis of the following areas:

* **Market**
* **Order Pad**
* **Account**

As a Business Analyst, I was involved in analyzing business requirements, reviewing existing application functionality and workflows, conducting competitor benchmarking, translating requirements into functional specifications, reviewing UI/UX designs, and validating the implemented solution through UAT.

My role involved working closely with UI/UX designers, IT and QA teams, and external vendors throughout the enhancement process.

# 1. Market

## Business Challenge

The existing Market section presented several types of market information within a relatively combined structure.

The existing experience included:

* Indices
* Bonds
* Interest Rates
* Market Movers
* Currency
* News

While users could access the required information, the presentation could be improved to make different categories of market information easier to understand and navigate.

The iSpeed 2.0 enhancement therefore focused on improving the **information structure, visual presentation, and accessibility of market-related information**.

---

## My BA Contribution

I reviewed the existing Market experience and analyzed how market information could be structured more clearly.

I also conducted competitor benchmarking, with **Moomoo** being one of the investment applications reviewed as a reference for market information presentation and user experience.

Based on the requirements, existing system structure, and benchmarking findings, several improvements were introduced.

### 1. Improved Market Information Structure

The existing combination of:

* Indices
* Bonds
* Interest Rates

was reorganized into clearer categories in the new application.

This provided a more structured way for users to distinguish between different types of market information.

---

### 2. Market Overview

A new **Market Overview** experience was introduced to provide users with a higher-level view of overall market information.

The Market Overview brought together relevant market data in a more centralized presentation.

It also supported the presentation of **AI-powered stock information through Bridgewise**, providing users with additional information when reviewing stocks.

---

### 3. Investment Theme

An **Investment Theme** feature was also introduced as part of the enhanced Market experience.

This provided an additional way for users to explore stocks based on investment themes rather than relying only on traditional market categories.

---

### 4. Market Movers, Currency & News

The presentation of existing functions was also redesigned.

Areas enhanced included:

* Market Movers
* Currency
* News

The focus was not only on visual changes, but also on improving how information was organized and presented within the application.

---

### 5. In-App Reports

Another enhancement was the introduction of **reports directly within the application**.

Previously, users were redirected to a website to access the details of reports.

The enhanced experience allowed users to access and view relevant report content more directly within the application, reducing the need to leave the app during their research journey.

# 2. Order Pad

## Business Challenge

The Order Pad was redesigned as part of the iSpeed 2.0 modernization effort.

The objective was not simply to refresh the UI, but to make the order-entry experience more informative and efficient by introducing additional functionality and improving how trading information was presented to users.

---

## My BA Contribution

I analyzed the existing Order Pad workflow and identified areas where additional information and functionality could improve the order-entry experience.

My work focused on:

* UI modernization
* Order information presentation
* Price-related functionality
* Estimated order value
* Maximum purchasable quantity
* Market depth information
* Currency-related funding scenarios

---

### 1. Auto-Calculation

An **auto-calculation function** was introduced into the Order Pad.

The enhanced experience provided users with calculated information such as:

* Estimated order value
* Maximum quantity they could purchase based on available funds

This reduced the need for users to manually calculate these values before submitting an order.

As part of the BA work, I reviewed the required inputs, expected calculation behaviour, and how the information should be presented within the Order Pad.

---

### 2. Market Depth-Based Price

The Order Pad also introduced a change to how the price was presented to users.

Previously, the system used the **Last Price** as the default reference.

The enhanced experience used information from the **current Market Depth** to prefill the relevant price.

I reviewed the expected workflow and how this information should be reflected in the Order Pad UI.

---

### 3. Multi-Currency Funding Scenario

One of the more complex areas involved users placing orders using **MYR** when they did not have sufficient MYR funds.

Previously, when users lacked sufficient MYR to place an order, the system would direct them to make a deposit.

However, users may have sufficient funds in another currency and could potentially use those funds for the transaction.

The existing system had limitations around automatically handling this scenario.

To support the enhanced experience, I analyzed the business requirement and proposed **three possible scenarios** for handling the currency conversion when users chose to place an order using another available currency.

The proposed logic considered situations where the system would automatically convert the required amount into MYR before proceeding with the order.

This required analysis of:

* Available currency balances
* Required MYR amount
* Currency conversion
* Insufficient MYR scenarios
* User-selected currency
* Expected system behaviour

I worked with the relevant stakeholders to clarify the scenarios and translate them into functional requirements for implementation.

# 3. Account

## Business Challenge

The Account section required a broader redesign to improve how users managed and viewed their financial activities.

Rather than changing only individual screens, the enhancement covered several **cash and portfolio-related workflows**, including:

* Deposit
* Currency Exchange
* Transaction History
* Share Transfer
* Cash & Shares
* Portfolio Holdings

The objective was to provide a more structured and consistent experience across these related activities.

---

## My BA Contribution

I reviewed the existing Account experience and analyzed how the different workflows were currently structured.

I also conducted competitor benchmarking to understand how similar investment applications organized functions such as deposit, currency exchange, share transfer, and portfolio management.

The findings were used as references when defining the proposed workflow and UI improvements.

---

### 1. Cash & Shares Visualization

A new **doughnut chart** was introduced to provide a visual representation of the user's holdings.

The visualization presented:

* Cash
* Shares
* ETF

This provided users with a clearer high-level view of the composition of their account.

---

### 2. Portfolio Presentation

The presentation of portfolio holdings was also redesigned.

Changes included improvements to:

* How Cash and Shares were presented
* How holdings were organized
* How stock information was displayed
* How users navigated within their portfolio

The focus was to make account information easier to scan and understand.

---

### 3. Cash Activity Workflows

A major part of the Account enhancement involved redesigning the **cash activity workflows**.

This included:

* Deposit
* Currency Exchange
* Transaction History
* Share Transfer

---

### 4. Overall Workflow & UI Modernization

The key improvement across the Account section was that these functions were not treated as isolated screens.

The project involved reviewing the **end-to-end workflow and relationship between different account activities**, then translating the requirements into a more consistent application experience.

My involvement included reviewing:

* Existing workflows
* Proposed workflows
* UI designs
* Functional requirements
* Navigation and information presentation
* Expected system behaviour
