# iSpeed 2.0 Application Enhancement

**Role:** Business Analyst
**Industry:** FinTech
**Project Type:** Application Enhancement / UI & UX Modernization

---

## Project Overview

iSpeed 2.0 was an application enhancement initiative focused on modernizing the existing trading application experience, improving information presentation, introducing new functionality, and simplifying selected user workflows.

As a Business Analyst, I was responsible for analyzing requirements, reviewing existing application functionality and workflows, benchmarking other investment applications, translating business needs into functional requirements, and validating the proposed UI/UX and system behaviour.

My main areas of responsibility were:

* Market / Market Data
* Order Pad
* Account

> **Confidentiality Note:** This case study is based on my work experience and has been anonymized for portfolio purposes. No confidential company data, internal documents, customer information, or proprietary screenshots are included.

# Key Areas of Contribution

## 1. Market / Market Data

### Business Challenge

The existing Market section contained different types of market information within a relatively combined structure, including:

* Indices
* Bonds
* Interest Rates
* Market Movers
* News

The existing presentation made it difficult to clearly distinguish different categories of market information and resulted in a less intuitive user experience.

### My Contribution

I analyzed the existing Market section and reviewed how other investment applications presented market information, with **Moomoo** being one of the main references used for benchmarking.

Based on the requirements and benchmarking findings, the Market section was restructured to provide clearer categorization and presentation of market information.

Key enhancements included:

* Separating different market data categories
* Enhancing the Market Movers presentation
* Introducing a **Market Overview** page to provide an overall view of market data
* Supporting the presentation of **AI-powered stock data** through Bridgewise
* Providing access to relevant **reports directly within the Market section**

## 2. Order Pad

### Business Requirement

The Order Pad was enhanced to support additional trading scenarios for the HK and US markets and provide users with a clearer order-entry experience.

### My BA Contribution

I was responsible for the Order Pad area and worked on the requirements and functional presentation of the enhancements.

#### Additional Order Types

For the HK and US markets, additional order types were introduced, including:

- Market
- Stop Limit
- Stop Market

I analyzed how the additional order types should be incorporated into the existing Order Pad flow and supported the definition of the corresponding functional requirements and UI presentation.

#### Auto-Calculation Display

An auto-calculation display was introduced to provide users with calculated information during the order-entry process.

I reviewed the required information and how the calculation should be presented within the Order Pad experience.

#### Deposit / Currency Exchange Flow

A new funding flow was introduced to provide users with different options when funding their account.

Users could choose between:

**Deposit** or **Currency Exchange**

I analyzed the proposed flow and reviewed how the options should be presented within the application experience.

### BA Activities

My involvement included:

1. Analyzing requirements for the Order Pad enhancements
2. Reviewing the existing order-entry workflow
3. Defining functional requirements for additional order types
4. Reviewing how new functions should be presented in the UI
5. Reviewing the Deposit and Currency Exchange flow
6. Validating the proposed design against the requirements

# 3. Account

### Business Challenge

The Account section required changes to improve how users viewed their cash, shares, and portfolio holdings.

The project also required benchmarking against other investment applications to determine suitable ways to present functions such as deposit, currency exchange, and share transfer.

### My Contribution

I analyzed the existing Account experience and worked on the requirements and presentation of several enhancements.

### Cash & Shares Visualization

A new **doughnut chart** was introduced to provide a visual representation of the user's:

* Cash
* Shares
* ETF

This allowed users to understand the overall proportion of their account holdings more easily.

### Account & Portfolio Presentation

I also worked on changes to:

* How Cash and Shares were presented
* How portfolio holdings were displayed
* How stock holdings were organized within the Account experience

### Competitor Benchmarking

I reviewed other investment applications to understand how similar functions were presented and structured.

Areas reviewed included:

* Deposit
* Currency Exchange
* Share Transfer
* Portfolio Holdings

The benchmarking findings were used as references when discussing the proposed application experience and functional requirements.

### BA Skills Demonstrated

`Requirements Analysis` · `Competitor Benchmarking` · `Functional Analysis` · `UI/UX Analysis` · `Workflow Review`
